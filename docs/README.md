# EdgePilotAI Research for OpenAI and Codex

EdgePilotAI Research helps users discover, configure, and historically evaluate quantitative trading strategies in supported OpenAI and Codex workspaces.

## What Research supports

- strategy discovery and configuration;
- reviewed strategy-package installation;
- public market data on demand;
- local historical backtests;
- reports covering returns, drawdown, costs, trade count, and robustness;
- reproducible research inputs, parameters, and versions.

## Product boundary

Research does not require an EdgePilotAI login, connect a trading account, accept exchange credentials, or place orders. It supports research and historical validation only.
